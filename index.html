MUSESINK / HELP

One squad. One soundtrack.

  1. Create a room and share the invite code or link.
  2. Everyone clicks Enable My Audio once. Browser autoplay rules require a click.
  3. Add YouTube video links to the current game's queue.
  4. The DJ controls playback and game profile. Anyone in the room can click Take the Aux.
  5. Keep the embedded YouTube player available; MuseSink does not rip or separate YouTube audio.
